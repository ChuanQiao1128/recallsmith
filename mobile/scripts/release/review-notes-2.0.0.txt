The app works without signing in: new users pick a goal, then take a 5-card starter lesson that ends with 3 draws for that pack. The first launch works offline: the first cards of each deck ship inside the app, and the full deck downloads once the device is online. The demo account above is needed only for cloud sync and for reporting a problem with a card (and Me > My reports). The Mistake Book (Me tab > Mistake Book) arrived in 1.8.0; 1.9.0 added crash and performance reporting; 2.0.0 comes with the rebuilt .NET & C# Interview deck (it replaces the earlier C# / .NET deck and is served from our content server), and the first cards of each deck now ship inside the app. 2.0.0 also adds FSRS review scheduling, Progress by domain and plainer wording across the app.

Premium subscription: not offered in 2.0.0. There is no premium content yet (all three decks are free), so the purchase screen is switched off by remote configuration and no in-app purchase is part of this submission. The previously approved Premium subscription product stays in App Store Connect for future premium decks. The description keeps the Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy links, and the Privacy Policy URL field in App Information points to the same policy.

Crash and performance diagnostics (since 1.9.0): the app sends crash reports and performance timings to Sentry, our error-monitoring provider. They contain no account data or email address; native crash reports carry a random ID that Sentry creates for the app install. A remote kill switch can turn the reporting off without an app update.

Report a card (since 1.9.0): sign in with the demo account, then either open a card you own in the Library, tap Show answer, and use "Report a problem" under the answer, or start a review and tap "Report" under the card on a recall check after you reveal the answer (a new card's first study view has no Report button). Reports appear under Me > My reports. A report is a private message to the developer: it is never shown to other users, so no user-generated content is published in the app.

Anonymous usage counts (new in 2.0.0): Settings > Privacy > "Share anonymous usage counts" (on by default) lets the app send a one-time record of early milestones (finishing setup, opening a first pack, starting or finishing sign-up, opening the app again a day or a week later), each with its dates, the deck, the platform and the app version. No account, email address, device or install ID is sent, and turning the switch off stops sending.

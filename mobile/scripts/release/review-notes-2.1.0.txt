The app works without signing in: new users pick a goal, then take a 5-card starter lesson. Each pack gets a one-time grant of 5 draws (the first time it is opened, or when its first lesson ends). The first cards of the AWS Associate Architect and Claude decks ship inside the app, so a first lesson on them works offline; the full decks download once the device is online. The demo account above is needed only for cloud sync and for reporting a problem with a card (and Me > My reports).

What is new in 2.1.0: swipe left or right on the Home cover to switch packs; AWS Architect Basics (the Chinese foundation deck) has its own name and cover; Open up to 5 packs at once or Open 1; installed decks stay ready after an app update; the .NET & C# Interview deck is no longer offered. Settings > Study > Card language (English / 中文) switches a card to its built-in Chinese translation where a deck includes one; no deck ships a translation yet, so in this version the setting has no visible effect and no translation toggle appears on cards.

Premium subscription: not offered in 2.1.0. There is no premium content (all three decks are free), so the purchase screen is switched off by remote configuration and no in-app purchase is part of this submission. The description keeps the Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy links, and the Privacy Policy URL field in App Information points to the same policy.

Crash and performance diagnostics: the app sends crash reports and performance timings to Sentry, our error-monitoring provider. They contain no account data or email address; native crash reports carry a random ID that Sentry creates for the app install. A remote kill switch can turn the reporting off without an app update.

Report a card: sign in with the demo account, then either open a card you own in the Library, tap Show answer, and use "Report a problem" under the answer, or start a review and tap "Report" under the card on a recall check after you reveal the answer (a new card's first study view has no Report button). Reports appear under Me > My reports. A report is a private message to the developer: it is never shown to other users, so no user-generated content is published in the app.

Anonymous usage counts: Settings > Privacy > "Share anonymous usage counts" (on by default) lets the app send a one-time record of early milestones (finishing setup, opening a first pack, starting or finishing sign-up, opening the app again a day or a week later), each with its dates, the deck, the platform and the app version. No account, email address, device or install ID is sent, and turning the switch off stops sending.

Delete account: Me > Settings > Account > Delete account. Following it permanently deletes the demo account (its server data and its sign-in), so please use it last; we re-create the demo account after review before it is used again.

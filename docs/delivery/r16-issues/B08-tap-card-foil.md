# B08 — TapCard.tsx (extracted from DrawCeremonyScreen: lift/flip/land, 90 ms queue, RAR/LEG focus fly-in, GH Pan tilt) + FoilLayer.tsx replacing HolographicLayer (fixes the useClock/useDerivedValue crash) (`tap-card-foil`)

The tap-to-flip card becomes its own Reanimated-on-the-guard component with a lift → flip → land sequence, rarity-scaled durations, a 90 ms tap queue, a focus fly-in for RAR/LEG and a GH Pan tilt; the per-card Skia shimmer that crashes the table at mount is replaced by `FoilLayer`, one Reanimated-driven canvas mounted only on the focused card; `HolographicLayer.tsx` shrinks to a compatibility shim over `FoilLayer` so the unchanged screen keeps compiling until B09 rewires it; the `tapCard*` styles move into the new `ceremonyStyles.ts`.

## Context

The table is dead code on every device with Lottie installed and would crash the moment it was reached: `HolographicLayer.tsx:65-72` destructures `useClock` and `useDerivedValue` from the Skia module, but `useDerivedValue` is not a Skia export and `useClock` (`node_modules/@shopify/react-native-skia/lib/module/external/reanimated/interpolators.js:27`) goes through Skia's ReanimatedProxy, which throws when Reanimated is not wired — `SkiaShimmer` calls both at `:74-82`, and the table's `TapCard` mounts a `HolographicLayer` on the front face of every RAR/LEG card (`DrawCeremonyScreen.tsx:367-375`), one canvas per card (`:370-375`; design §3.5 bans "one Canvas per TapCard (today N useClock canvases, :367-383)", `docs/release-1.6.0-plan-2026-09-19.md:133`). `SkiaBurst` (`:113-179`) calls `useDerivedValue` inside a `.map` (`:166-171`, hooks in a loop) and is mounted nowhere. Design §3.7 row `src/components/HolographicLayer.tsx` (`:165`) says replace it; the DoD (`:187`) says "HolographicLayer shimmer replaced by FoilLayer; exactly one Skia Canvas per stage plus at most one focused-card canvas; SkiaBurst deleted".

The flip itself is the prototype motion the audit calls out (`release-1.6.0-plan:38`, "a pure rotateY with a hard face swap and nothing else moving"): `DrawCeremonyScreen.tsx:147-388` is an RN `Animated` `TapCard` whose press either snaps (`:179-183`, the `!hasAnimated` branch) or springs `flipRef` 0 → 1 (`:191-206`, `friction: 6, tension: 80`) after a 200 ms `setTimeout` tease for rare cards (`:190-199`); the two faces cross-fade at 0.49/0.5 (`:253-260`) while rotating (`:245-252`); there is no lift, no land, no per-rarity duration, no queue, and `testID={`tap-card-${index}`}` exists only on the animated branch (`:279`), not on the static one (`:224-242`). Storyboard S6 (`release-1.6.0-plan:102`) replaces it: "per tap: lift 80 → flip 380 (COM) / 480 (RAR) / 640 (LEG) → land 200 → rimSettleMs 800/1000/1200 … lift (translateY −10, scale 1.08 …) then rotateY 0 → 180° with Easing.bezier(0.05,0.7,0.1,1) (replaces spring :191-206 and the symmetric bezier :654); faces swap at 90° (opacity step at 0.5 kept for the test path)"; M6 (`:116`) adds "queued taps 90 ms apart … RAR/LEG focus-fly to the centre for legibility + tilt foil; COM stays matte with no fly"; §2 `:70` "Each tap flips ONE card in 350–500 ms with its own rarity glow visible behind it"; `:73` "impact on the flip frame scaled by rarity … a soft impact per …".

The contract for both components, `createTapQueue`, the accessibility props and the shim is `docs/delivery/r16-issues/B00-contracts.md` §2.11 (`:509-545`) and is reproduced verbatim below; `ceremonyStyles.ts` is created here (not by B11) because of B00 §9 #4 (`:817`) — B09 must not import a `Lottie`-named file. Everything animated goes through the B02 guard (`mobile/src/components/ceremony/reanimatedGuard.ts`, B00 §2.1 `:107-186`): under vitest `motionAvailable === false`, `skiaAvailable === false`, `SkiaModule === null`, `Reanimated.useSharedValue(v)` is a `useRef({ value: v })`, `useAnimatedStyle(fn)` is `fn()` evaluated per render, every `with*` returns its final target and `GestureHandler.PanHost` (the guarded gesture host — B00 §2.1 names it `PanHost`, an alias for the library's `GestureDetector` — use the alias) renders its children unchanged — so the same `TapCard` serves both the Skia renderer and the fallback renderer (B00 §3.5 `:660`), and the unit test asserts behaviour through props and rendered output, not through animation. Haptics (B04, B00 §2.7 `:396-423`) are a dep because the one haptic only the card can time — the LEG heavy impact at landing — is fired from the card's land callback; every tap-frame cue stays with the screen's `onTapStart`.

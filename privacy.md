# RecallSmith – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2025‑XX‑XX_  
(Replace this date when you update the policy.)

This Privacy Policy describes how **RecallSmith** (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use our mobile application (“the App”).

The short version:

> This first version of RecallSmith does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.  
> All study progress and settings are stored **locally on your device**.

If we make material changes in the future (for example, adding accounts or cloud sync), this document will be updated and the changes will be clearly communicated.

---

## 1. Information We Do **Not** Collect

In this version of the App, we do **not**:

- Collect your name, email address, or other contact info;
- Collect your device identifiers for tracking or analytics;
- Collect your location;
- Collect your contacts, photos, or other personal files;
- Use third‑party analytics SDKs;
- Use advertising SDKs or show ads.

We also do not create server‑side user profiles.

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores some data **locally** on your device in order to function:

- **Deck content** downloaded for study (e.g. questions, explanations, code snippets);
- **Study progress**, such as:
  - Which cards you have reviewed;
  - Your review history and ratings (Again / Hard / Good / Easy);
  - The next review time for each card;
- **Local settings**, such as:
  - How many cards to study in a session;
  - Basic preferences related to review and scheduling.

This information is stored using the mobile platform’s local storage (for example, AsyncStorage on iOS) and is **not transmitted to our servers** in this version of the App.

If you delete the App, this local data may be removed by the operating system as part of the uninstall process.

---

## 3. Network Requests and Remote Content

The App may make simple network requests for:

1. **Remote configuration**

   The App occasionally fetches a small configuration file (for example, a JSON file hosted on a server such as GitHub).  
   This file may contain:

   - The latest available app version;
   - The minimum supported app version;
   - A link to the App Store page, used for update prompts;
   - Information about available decks or content updates.

   These requests do **not** include personal identifiers such as name or email.  
   Standard technical information (such as IP address) may be visible to the server that hosts the file, as with any web request.

2. **Downloading deck content**

   When new or updated study decks become available, the App may download them from a server or file storage service.  
   Again, we do not attach personal identifiers to these requests.

We do not use these network requests to track individual users across apps or services.

---

## 4. Third‑Party Services

In this version, RecallSmith does **not** integrate:

- Third‑party analytics SDKs;
- Advertising networks;
- Social login (e.g. “Sign in with Apple”, Google, Facebook, etc.).

If we add any third‑party services in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe:

- What is integrated;
- What data is shared (if any);
- For what purpose.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

RecallSmith is designed for adult users and developers preparing for technical interviews.  
We do **not** knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that the App has inadvertently collected personal information from a child, we will delete that information as soon as reasonably possible.

---

## 6. Security

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on our servers in this version, the main security concern is the **physical security of your device**.

To protect your local data:

- Use a device passcode, Touch ID, or Face ID;
- Keep your operating system and apps up to date;
- Only install the App from official app stores (e.g. Apple App Store).

If future versions introduce accounts and cloud features, we will describe server‑side security practices in an updated version of this policy.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.  
When we do:

- We will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this document;
- In case of material changes (for example, adding account registration or cloud sync), we will clearly communicate this in the App or in the release notes.

Your continued use of the App after changes have been published will constitute your acceptance of the updated policy.
